1 · NOWY LIPOWIEC, POLAND — FRIDAY LEAD STORY · NATO AIRSPACE BREACHED

Russian missiles land in Poland

Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland on Friday, during a huge Russian attack on Ukraine. Warsaw called it a deliberate provocation; NATO’s secretary-general called it a grave violation of allied airspace.

A Kh-101 cruise missile crashed beside a forest at Nowy Lipowiec, about 50 km from the Ukrainian border. Two more objects fell near Rudnik nad Sanem and Mołodycz. No one in Poland was hurt.

Polish F-16s and Swedish Gripens patrolled with Patriot batteries on alert — but no one fired, as crews had about three minutes to judge tracks they could not fully identify. Poland will ask NATO for reinforcements.

2
KYIV · UKRAINE

Mass strike hits Kyiv bridges and power

Russia fired more than 400 drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine overnight, hours after Putin vowed retaliation for the Crimea Bridge attack. At least nine people were killed; three bridges over the Dnipro were hit and emergency power cuts spread across 11 regions.

3
GAZA CITY · GAZA

Israeli strikes kill six despite ceasefire

Israeli strikes killed six people in Gaza on Friday, civil defence said, a day after Israel completed a rare tank incursion in the centre of the strip. More than 1,600 Palestinians have been killed since the October 2025 ceasefire took effect.

4
WASHINGTON · UNITED STATES

Trump’s 90-minute press conference

Fresh from his Asia trip, President Trump held a freewheeling 90-minute briefing, promising to penalise US firms that build data centres abroad and to veto Democratic bills when they take Congress in 2027 — while offering to deal on health care.

5
NEW YORK · MARKETS

S&P and Dow slide into correction

Wall Street plunged on Friday as the AI sell-off gathered pace: the S&P 500 fell 3.7% and the Nasdaq 5.3%, both now in correction. Nvidia sank 14% in a day; Anthropic fell 9% after its record IPO debut.

6
BRUSSELS · EUROPE

UK and EU seal post-Brexit reset deal

Britain and the EU agreed a reset deal at their Brussels summit: most food checks dropped, a youth mobility scheme for 100,000 people a year, and linked carbon markets. University fee cuts were put off for six months.

7
SHENZHEN · CHINA

Trump, Xi and Putin’s first trilateral

The first-ever meeting of the three leaders ended with a four-point declaration toward peace — but few concrete results on Ukraine or Iran. Trump said Putin told him he wants the war to end by diplomacy.

8Peace talks edge closer. US envoys see progress on a Ukraine peace framework, with Washington open to NATO-style guarantees; envoy Jared Kushner said Moscow may accept them. Russia ruled out Ukraine joining NATO.

12Pretoria protest targets Nigerians. An anti-migrant group marched in Pretoria on Wednesday demanding a ban on Nigerians entering South Africa; a Nigerian lawyer in Johannesburg said the group has no authority to demand one.
13New Zealand still undecided. Two weeks after the election, no government is formed: Labour narrowly won the party vote, National won more seats, and about 400,000 special votes are counted on 27 November.
14US backs coca spraying. Washington backed Colombia’s plan to resume aerial spraying of coca crops, despite concerns from US lawmakers — a shift in the anti-drug strategy.
15Brazil’s central bank steps in. The bank sold $1 billion of reserves to slow the real’s slide against the dollar, amid market worries about government spending after Lula’s re-election.
16Argentina passes disability law. The Senate voted 71–0 on Thursday for a permanent disability benefits law, replacing the emergency scheme — a pension worth 70% of the minimum retirement benefit.
17Storm floods Gaza tents. The season’s first strong storm flooded about 70,000 tents in Gaza within 12 hours, killing at least five people; heavy rain has directly affected at least 106,000 people, UNICEF said.
18Helicopters collide at Everest. An Indian tourist died after two helicopters collided near the Mount Everest base camp, officials confirmed to the BBC.
19Kim crosses new bridge to China. North Korea’s Kim Jong Un arrived in the Chinese border city of Dandong on Saturday, crossing via the newly opened New Yalu River Bridge, Yonhap reported.
